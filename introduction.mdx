Guardian Defence is built on one clear philosophy: physical techniques are always the last resort. True self-defence begins long before a confrontation turns physical — it starts with situational awareness, continues through verbal de-escalation, and only reaches physical action when every other option has been exhausted. This guide gives you the knowledge to operate confidently at every stage of that continuum, from reading a room to responding to an imminent threat, while keeping safety, legality, and ethics at the centre of every decision you make.

## How to Use This Guide

Work through Guardian Defence in order the first time through. The sections are sequenced deliberately — each one builds on the last.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start With Safety Principles">
    Read the [Safety Principles](/safety-principles) page before anything else. Understanding universal safety rules is a prerequisite for every technique that follows, not an optional extra.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Study De-Escalation">
    Spend serious time in the de-escalation section. Most real-world confrontations can be resolved — or avoided entirely — through awareness and communication. Physical skills matter far less often than people assume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Physical Discipline">
    Once you have the foundation in place, move into the physical discipline most relevant to your life and circumstances: firearms, edged-weapon defence, or unarmed techniques. Work through each section from its introduction page forward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Train Under Qualified Supervision">
    Use this guide as a reference and study companion, not a substitute for proper instruction. Book a certified course, find a qualified instructor, and practise consistently in a controlled environment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who This Guide Is For

Guardian Defence is written for anyone who takes personal safety seriously and wants a reliable, well-structured reference to support their training.

**Home defenders** will find practical frameworks for securing their environment, understanding use-of-force thresholds, and building firearm safety habits that protect the whole household. **Outdoor enthusiasts** — hikers, hunters, and backcountry travellers — will benefit from the situational awareness and wildlife-encounter de-escalation material alongside the firearms handling sections. **Security professionals** working in private or corporate roles will find the takedown, control, and de-escalation content particularly applicable to their daily responsibilities. **Responsible firearm owners** at any experience level can use the firearms section as both a refresher and a deeper reference on safe storage, handling, and defensive use.

Whatever your background, this guide assumes you are here to learn, train responsibly, and prioritise the safety of yourself and everyone around you.
