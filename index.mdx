Welcome to Guardian Defence — the complete reference for building real-world self-defence knowledge and skills. Whether you are a first-time firearm owner, a martial arts practitioner, or a security professional, this guide walks you through every critical discipline: situational awareness, de-escalation, firearms handling, edged-weapon tactics, and unarmed takedowns. Everything here is built on a single foundation — responsible, lawful, and ethical self-defence.

<Note>
  Self-defence knowledge is most powerful when you never need to use it physically. Always prioritise de-escalation, disengagement, and legal compliance over physical confrontation. The techniques in this guide are a last resort, not a first response.
</Note>

## How to Get Started

Follow these steps to get the most out of Guardian Defence, regardless of your experience level.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the Safety Principles">
    Before studying any technique, read the [Safety Principles](/safety-principles) page cover to cover. Every discipline in this guide depends on a non-negotiable safety foundation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Understand De-Escalation First">
    Work through the de-escalation section before any physical discipline. The ability to avoid a confrontation is always superior to winning one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Area of Study">
    Select the discipline most relevant to your situation — firearms, edged-weapon defence, or unarmed techniques — and work through it systematically from the introductory pages forward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Practice With Professional Supervision">
    No written guide replaces hands-on training. Take a certified course, work with a qualified instructor, and never practise full-force techniques unsupervised.
  </Step>
</Steps>
